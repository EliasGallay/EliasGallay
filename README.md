## 🚀 Featured project

### [Solicitudes de Insumos](https://github.com/EliasGallay/Solicitudes-Farmacia)

Supply-request and delivery system in production at the municipal health centers of Funes, Argentina. It replaced messages, spreadsheets and paper with a single traceable workflow: partial deliveries with delivery notes, item-by-item receipt confirmation, purchase requisitions, an immutable audit log and in-app notifications.

Business rules live in the database: transactional RPCs with row locking, computed states, and role- and area-based Row Level Security.

`Next.js 16` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage)` · `Zod` · `Vitest` · `Vercel`
